# Central Securities Depositories (CSD)

Business context: Cash Management. Source classification: business role.

## Definition

Infrastructure that, holds or controls, the holding of physical or dematerialised financial instruments belonging to all, or a large portion of, the investors in a securities market. This affects the centralised transfer of ownership of such securities by entries on its books and records.
