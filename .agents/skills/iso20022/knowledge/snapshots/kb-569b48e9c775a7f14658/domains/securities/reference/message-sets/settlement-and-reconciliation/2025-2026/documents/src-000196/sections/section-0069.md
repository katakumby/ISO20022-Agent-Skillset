# Two Parties

**P361** Two Parties

**P362** This diagram report the messages flow between the securities financing instructing party (a broker for example) and the securities financing servicing party/settlement executing party (a global custodian for instance).

**P363** The securities financing instructing party sends a SecuritiesFinancingInstruction to the securities financing servicing party/settlement executing party.

**P364** The securities financing servicing party/settlement executing party sets up both the closing and the opening leg of the securities financing.

**P365** The securities financing servicing party/settlement executing party may send a processing and/or a matching or settlement status to the securities financing instructing party for the opening and the closing legs.

**P366** When the opening and closing legs settle, the securities financing servicing party/settlement executing party sends a SecuritiesFinancingConfirmation to the securities financing instructing party.
