**P940** Transparency of Holdings Statement 

**P941** An account servicer, such as a custodian, sends the SecuritiesBalanceTransparencyReport statement message to a receiver, who in turn, may enrich it with data of its own and send the aggregated statement onward in the custody chain. 

**P942** The receiver of the message responds with the SecuritiesBalanceTransparencyReportStatusAdvice message to accept or reject the statement of holdings as presented in the aggregated statement.
