**P632** Transaction Generation

**P633** In this scenario, an executing/servicing party sends a SecuritiesSettlementTransactionGenerationNotification to an instructing party to advise the instructing party of a securities settlement transaction that has been generated/created by the executing/servicing party for the instructing party.

**P634** The reason for creation can range from the automatic transformation of pending settlement instructions following a corporate event to the recovery of an account owner transactions' database initiated by its executing/servicing party.
