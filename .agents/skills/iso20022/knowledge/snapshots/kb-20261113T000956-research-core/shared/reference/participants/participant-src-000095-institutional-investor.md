# Institutional Investor

Business context: Investment Funds. Source classification: business role.

## Definition

Organisation whose primary purpose is to invest its own assets or those held in trust by it for others. Includes investment companies, mutual funds, brokerages, insurance companies, pension funds, investment banks.
