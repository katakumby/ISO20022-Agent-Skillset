**P262** Confirmation and Reversal

**P263** The scenarios developed in this section illustrate the corporate action messages flow during the corporate action confirmation and reversal phases. For non-elective corporate action events, this phase comes directly after the announcement and final entitlement phases. For elective corporate action events, this phase comes directly after the election phase.
