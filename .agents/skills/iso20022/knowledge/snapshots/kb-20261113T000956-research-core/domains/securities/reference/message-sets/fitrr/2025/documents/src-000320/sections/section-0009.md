**P86** Scope

**P87** With the coming into force of MiFIR and MiFID II, market participants are required to report to national competent authorities all data pertaining to financial instruments - equity, non-equity, transactions and reference data - traded or admitted to trading within the European Economic Area (EEA) as well as transactions in these instruments. Through the use of delegation agreements by the NCAs, all data will be routed to ESMA (directly and indirectly) where the various message related operations required by MiFIR and MiFID II will be performed.

**P88** Based on the large volumes of data as well as the number and range of institutions that will generate and submit data, the use of a single reporting standard has been decided, which will support and aid high levels of automation and data distribution.
