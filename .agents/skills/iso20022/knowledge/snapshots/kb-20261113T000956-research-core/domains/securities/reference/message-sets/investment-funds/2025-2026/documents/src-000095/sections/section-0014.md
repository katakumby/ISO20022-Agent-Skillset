**P123** Fund Reference Data

**P124** The FundReferenceDataReport message is used to convey the key reference data for financial instruments needed to facilitate trading. The message may also include reporting data concerning product governance, such as target market data, and a breakdown of the costs and fees as required by MiFID II and as specified in the European MiFID Template (EMT) document. The report may be requested with the InvestmentFundReportRequest.
