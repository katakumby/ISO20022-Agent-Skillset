**P94** Related Messages

**P95** The FIToFIPaymentReversal message is used to reverse a payment previously executed.

**P96** The PaymentReturn message is used to undo a payment previously settled.

**P97** The FIToFIPaymentStatusReport message is used to inform on the positive or negative status of an instruction (either single or file) and to report on a pending instruction.

**P98** The FIToFIPaymentStatusRequest message is used to request a FIToFIPaymentStatusReport message containing information on the status of a previously sent instruction.
