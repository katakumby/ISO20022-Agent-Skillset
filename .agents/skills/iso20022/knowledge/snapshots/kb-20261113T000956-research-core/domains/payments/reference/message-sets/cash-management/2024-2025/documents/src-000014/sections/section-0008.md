**P65** Background

**P66** This Message Definition Report covers a set of thirty-five ISO 20022 MessageDefinitions, developed by SWIFT in close collaboration with cash market infrastructures and cash service providers and approved by the ISO 20022 Payments Standards Evaluation Group (SEG) in February 2025.

**P67** These MessageDefinitions are specifically designed to support payment transactions intra-day in real-time to address the increase regulatory pressure on managing settlement risk, monitoring the centralisation of liquidity management and treasury functions and the integration of securities settlement systems with payment settlement systems.
