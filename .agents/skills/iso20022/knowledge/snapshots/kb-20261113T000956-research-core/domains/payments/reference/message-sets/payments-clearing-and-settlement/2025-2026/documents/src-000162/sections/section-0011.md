**P89** Instruction Messages

**P90** The FIToFICustomerCreditTransfer message is used to move funds from a debtor account to a creditor.

**P91** The FIToFICustomerDirectDebit message is used to collect funds from a debtor account for a creditor.

**P92** The FinancialInstitutionCreditTransfer message is used to move funds from a debtor account to a creditor, where both debtor and creditor are financial institutions.

**P93** The FinancialInstitutionDirectDebit message is used to collect funds from a debtor account for a creditor, where both debtor and creditor are financial institutions.
