**P572** Reconciliation and Reporting

**P573** This section describes the various reports an account servicer can provide to the account owner or designated agent.

**P574** For instance these reports can provide at a specified moment in time:

**P575** the details of pending increases and decreases of holdings, for all or selected securities in a specified safekeeping account, for all or selected reasons why the transaction is pending

**P576** the details of any increases and/or decreases of holdings, which may have occurred over a specified period of time, for all or a selected quantity of securities in the requested safekeeping account, which the account servicer 

**P577** holds for the account owner

**P578** the quantity and identification of financial instruments that the account servicer maintains for the account owner

**P579** the history of all the statuses, modifications, replacement and cancellation of a specific transaction during its whole life cycle
