**P176** Description of BusinessActivities

**P177** This section presents the different BusinessActivities within each BusinessProcess. BusinessActivities of a process are described in swim lane diagrams and are referred in this document as activity diagrams.

**P178** The development of an activity diagram is part of the ISO 20022 modelling process and allows capturing the requirements.

**P179** The activity diagram provides a zoom-in on the BusinessActivities taking place during each of the BusinessProcesses described in Section 4. It also shows the BusinessActivities that are triggered when another BusinessActivity has a negative result.

**P180** What is the activity diagram about?

**P181** It is a diagram representing the ‘common lifecycle’ of a BusinessProcess

**P182** A start point  shows where the lifecycle of the business process commences and the end points show  where the lifecycle may possibly end

[fig-0003 - original figure evidence](../diagrams/fig-0003.md)

**P183** A lozenge      means a that a choice between several actions can be made

[fig-0004 - original figure evidence](../diagrams/fig-0004.md)

**P184** A bar means a that several actions are initiated in parallel

[fig-0005 - original figure evidence](../diagrams/fig-0005.md)

**P185** The flow of activities between the involved Participants (parties)

**P186** BusinessActivities may result in different actions, that is, information is conveyed from one party to another party.

**P187** Both in scope and out of scope activities are included, with a different level of details. There are no information requirements for out of scope activities, except that they should be clearly identified in the diagram.

**P188** Activity diagrams are always accompanied with a text describing the BusinessActivities and their interactions.
