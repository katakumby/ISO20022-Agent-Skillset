**P345** Securities Financing Instruction and Confirmation

**P346** This section illustrates the instruction, status and confirmation of the opening and closing leg of a securities financing transaction and the relevant messages exchanged between the securities financing instructing party, the securities financing servicing party and the settlement executing party. The pure settlement of the opening and closing leg, when no securities financing information is required, would still be handled using regular settlement messages. The flows apply to repo/reverse repo, securities lending, securities borrowing, collateral in and collateral out (managed by a securities financing servicing party).
