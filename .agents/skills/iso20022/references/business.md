# Business meaning

Resolve the collection and exact message when applicable. Follow business.md, mdr-evidence.md, roles.md, source sections, flows and scoped participants. Many message indexes are navigation pages rather than full business descriptions.

Use MDR text for purpose, actors, responsibilities, process sequencing and business rules. An XML name or containment relation alone cannot establish a sender, owner, process or business condition. Separate statements about the requested message from related-message or ambiguous pronoun clauses.

Rules are often grouped in Constraints source sections. Preserve preconditions, exceptions and cross-field dependencies. Do not restrict rule searches to the pilot's individual business-rule pages.

Keep account owner, account servicer and other participant roles scoped to their publication and relationship. Similar labels across collections do not establish equivalence. Source examples may use historical message versions; report that distinction.

For a flow, retrieve canonical diagram Markdown or Mermaid text and its entity/relationship description. Preserve origin, direction, actors, boundaries, cardinalities and notes. Retain any textual limitation about a feature Mermaid cannot express.

An explanatory Mermaid diagram may summarize cited evidence; label it as derived and validate it locally with a compatible renderer when available. Do not claim source-artwork comparison without actual requested inspection, or renderer validation when unavailable. Prefer returning an existing validated Mermaid block for a simple request.
