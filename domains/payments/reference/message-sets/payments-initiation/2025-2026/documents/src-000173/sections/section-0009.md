**P80** Scope

**P81** The scope covers a set of messages, exchanged between a debtor and its bank or between a creditor and its bank, to initiate, collect, manage and monitor payments.

**P82** The CustomerCreditTransferInitiation and CustomerDirectDebitInitiation messages described in this document can be used for initiating either multiple payment orders or single transfers.
