**P83** Request 

**P84** AccountReportingRequest

**P85** This message is sent by the account owner, either directly or through a forwarding agent, to one of its account servicing institutions. It is used to ask the account servicing institution to send a report on the account owner's account in a BankToCustomerAccountReport, a BankToCustomerStatement or a BankToCustomerDebitCreditNotification.
