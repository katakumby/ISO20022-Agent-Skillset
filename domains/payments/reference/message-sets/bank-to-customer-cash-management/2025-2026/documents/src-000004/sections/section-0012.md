# Reporting 

Source: [src-000004](../../../../../../../../../raw-input/banktocustomer_cash_management/ISO20022_MDRPart1_BankToCustomerCashManagement_2025_2026_v2.docx). Locator: heading path Scope and Functionality / Groups of MessageDefinitions and Functionality / Groups / Reporting .

**P76** Reporting 

**P77** BankToCustomerAccountReport

**P78** This message is sent by the account servicer to an account owner or to a party authorised by the account owner to receive the message. It can be used to inform the account owner, or authorised party, of the entries reported to the account, and/or to provide the owner with balance information on the account at a given point in time.

**P79** BankToCustomerStatement

**P80** This message is sent by the account servicer to an account owner or to a party authorised by the account owner to receive the message. It is used to inform the account owner, or authorised party, of the entries booked to the account, and to provide the owner with balance information on the account at a given point in time.

**P81** BankToCustomerDebitCreditNotification

**P82** This message is sent by the account servicer to an account owner or to a party authorised by the account owner to receive the message. It can be used to inform the account owner, or authorised party, of single or multiple debit and/or credit entries reported to the account.
