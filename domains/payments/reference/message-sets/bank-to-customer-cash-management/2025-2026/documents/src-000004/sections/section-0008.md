**P68** Background

**P69** This Message Definition Report covers a set of four Message Definitions developed by SWIFT, on behalf of IFX, TWIST, OAGi and SWIFT, and approved by the ISO 20022 Payments Standards Evaluation Group (SEG) in January 2026.

**P70** These messages are specifically designed to support reporting of cash transactions between an account servicer and its customers.
