**P79** Groups

**P80** The messages of this Cash Management message set can be classified in four different categories:

**P81** Information messages: the real query and response messages, where the member sends a request for information about balances, transactions, limits and business data (for example, membership, day profiles, exchange rates) and the service provider sends a reply.

**P82** Action messages: sent by a member to request modifications or cancellations of transactions held at the account servicing institution, alter some characteristics of the transactions (for example, their priority) and upload business data (for example, limits, users' profiles).

**P83** Warnings messages: used by the service provider to warn users on an ad-hoc basis (for example, warning messages, temporary suspension of service).

**P84** Administration messages: used by the service provider to broadcast information to users, based on regular events (cut-off events, profiles of operational day)
