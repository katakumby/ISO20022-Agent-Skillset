**P65** Document Scope and Objectives

**P66** This document is the first part of the Message Definition Report (MDR) that describes the BusinessTransactions and underlying message set. For the sake of completeness, the document may also describe BusinessActivities that are not in the scope of the business processes covered in this document.

**P67** This document describes the following:

**P68** the BusinessProcess scope 

**P69** the BusinessRoles involved in these BusinessProcesses

**P71** The main objectives of this document are as follows:

**P72** to provide information about the messages that support the business processes

**P73** to explain the BusinessProcesses and BusinessActivities these messages have addressed

**P74** to give a high level description of BusinessProcesses and the associated BusinessRoles

**P75** to document the BusinessTransactions 

**P76** to provide business examples

**P78** The messages definitions are specified in Message Definition Report Part 2.
