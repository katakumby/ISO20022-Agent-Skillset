**P44** Background

**P45** This Message Definition Report covers a set of five MessageDefinitions developed by Banco de España and Banque de France on behalf of 4CB in close collaboration with SWIFT, submitted by Deutsche Bundesbank on behalf of 4CB and approved by Securities Standards Evaluation Groups (SEG) on 19 February 2026.

**P46** Originally, these messages have been designed to support the T2S community with retrieving securities settlement and securities position related information from the platform. T2S (TARGET2-Securities) is a European securities settlement engine which offers centralised settlement in central bank money across all European securities markets. The fundamental objective of T2S is to integrate and harmonise the highly fragmented securities settlement infrastructure in Europe. It aims to reduce the costs of cross-border securities settlement and increase competition and choice among providers of post-trading services in Europe.

**P47** The detailed description of each MessageDefinition is provided in Message Definition Report Part 2.
