**P156** Statement

**P157** Statement messages are used to report the holdings of or transactions on a specific account or sub-account on a specified date or period.

**P158** There are four types of statement: 

**P159** Accounting holdings statement,

**P160** Custody holdings statement,

**P161** Transactions statement,

**P162** Securities Balance Transparency Report.

**P163** Additional messages, for example, the statement of pending transactions, will be available in a subsequent phase of the funds standards initiative.
