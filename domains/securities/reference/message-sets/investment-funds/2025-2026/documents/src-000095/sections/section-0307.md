**P4849** Transfer - Matched Leg Transfer (MLT)

**P4850** In the matched leg transfer (MLT) process, two instructions are sent to the transfer agent, one to instruct the transfer out leg, and one to instruct the transfer in leg. The matched leg transfer (MLT) process may also be known as the double leg transfer (DLT) process.
