**P168** Securities Market Practice (SMPG) Investment Funds

**P169** The Securities Market Practice Group (SMPG) investment funds working group has published market practice for a number of investment fund business processes on the MyStandards platform, including collections for the order process and the single leg transfer process.

**P170** To view the market practice collections, search on 'SMPG IF'. Then select 'Groups'. Then select 'SMPG IF'. This provides a view of some of the investment fund market practice Usage Guidelines. Click on 'more guidelines' to see the complete set of investment fund market practice collections.
