**P4695** Transfer - Single Leg Transfer (SLT) 

**P4696** The single leg transfer (SLT) process is defined from the point of view of the transfer agent. The transfer agent will act upon the receipt of a single transfer instruction, the TransferOutInstruction (sese.001) message.
