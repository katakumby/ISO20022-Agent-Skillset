**P206** Proxy and In-Direct Reporting

**P207** This flow describes an end of day / end of reporting period operation where the SA upon checking all its' data per venue identifies issues (e.g. missed submissions, missed records) and distributes a Status Advice message, with status Reminder, to the CAs to route to venues which report via that CA. The status advice is generated at the level of an RE identifier e.g. segment MIC.
