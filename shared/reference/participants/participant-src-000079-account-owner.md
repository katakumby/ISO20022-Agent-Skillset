# Account Owner

Business context: Corporate Actions. Source classification: functional participant role.

## Definition

Party that legally holds the account or the party in a contractual relationship with the servicer.

Evidence: [T8](../../../domains/securities/reference/message-sets/corporate-actions/2025-2026/documents/src-000079/tables/tbl-0008.md), source row 3.

A functional participant role describes involvement in a relationship or transaction. It is not an assertion that a particular institution always plays that role.
