# Beneficial Owner

Business context: Investment Funds. Source classification: business role.

## Definition

Person(s) or entity entitled to the benefits of ownership even through another party such as a broker or bank (the nominal owner) and actually has possession and title to the security. (Source: http://www.rbeck.com).

Evidence: [T19](../../../domains/securities/reference/message-sets/investment-funds/2025-2026/documents/src-000095/tables/tbl-0019.md), source row 7.
