# Executing/Servicing Party

Business context: T2S SecuritiesAccountReferenceData. Source classification: functional participant role.

## Definition

Party that processes, monitors and reports on transactions received from the Instructing party.

Evidence: [T5](../../../domains/securities/reference/message-sets/t2s-securities-account-reference-data/december-2019/documents/src-000371/tables/tbl-0005.md), source row 3.

A functional participant role describes involvement in a relationship or transaction. It is not an assertion that a particular institution always plays that role.
