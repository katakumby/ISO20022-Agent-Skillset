# Instructing Party

Business context: T2S IntraBalanceMovement. Source classification: business role.

## Definition

Party that instructs the executing/servicing party to process and monitor a transaction.

Evidence: [T6](../../../domains/securities/reference/message-sets/t2s-intra-balance-movement/march-2024/documents/src-000359/tables/tbl-0006.md), source row 2.

A functional participant role describes involvement in a relationship or transaction. It is not an assertion that a particular institution always plays that role.
