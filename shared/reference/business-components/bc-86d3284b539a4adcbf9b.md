# BuyerProtectionInstruction

## Definition

Instruction submitted by the buyer of a financial instrument involved in a pending settlement transaction, requesting the seller (who is the holder on record) to act on their behalf in a voluntary corporate action. This instruction ensures the buyerâ€™s election is executed despite not being the legal holder at record date.
